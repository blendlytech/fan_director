# Fan Director Studio — creator-branded boutique

## Product
Design exploration, not production software. A fan collaboratively plans a custom creator commission. The AI Director helps clarify an idea, suggests only creator-approved catalog choices, explains deterministic price tradeoffs, and builds a Scene Card. Creator approves scope, price and delivery before payment. Payment and delivery stay on the creator's existing platform. No claims of integrated checkout or real AI in this mockup.

## Brand and style
Fictional demonstration creator: Maya Atelier. Clearly identify as demo. No real creator assets supplied. Use a typographic wordmark, tasteful nonexplicit still-life reference photography (vintage lounge, flowers, soft studio lighting), no invented creator portrait. Primary style source is Superdesign luxury-focused-design-system: warm cream, dusty rose, editorial whitespace, fine rules. Adapt it to the user's accepted boutique direction: Cormorant Garamond serif headings, Inter sans-serif UI; never League Spartan or giant travel hero elements. Background `#FDF8F3`; panel `#FFFFFF`; secondary `#F5F0EB`; primary text/CTA `#302720`; muted text `#70625C`; dusty rose `#E4A4BD`; deep rose `#84485D`; rules `#DED3CB`. No neon, gradients, glass effects, tiny unreadable utility text or low-contrast rose body text.

## Layout and components
Desktop workspace max-width 1320px, generous 32px margins, 12-column grid. Two main columns: Director conversation 7, live Scene Card 5. Restrained rounded corners 12px, subtle shadows, fine dividers. Serif title 44px, subheads 28px; UI 14–16px with 1.5 line height. Controls minimum 44px targets. Dark espresso primary buttons, cream secondary buttons, rose selected tiles with dark text and explicit selected indicator. Focus rings, semantic buttons and text labels, not color-only states. Mobile stacks panels with sticky estimate/Scene Card button; no horizontal overflow. Motion 160ms transitions, honor reduced-motion.

## Journey
Boutique entrance -> inspiration collection -> AI Director -> Scene Card review -> submitted -> creator review -> proposed changes or approval -> payment externally -> production -> delivered externally. Draft/submission/approval/payment are distinct. No payment is taken by this prototype. Mark estimated prices and timing subject to approval. Boundaries apply to every option; assistant cannot override them or invent prices.

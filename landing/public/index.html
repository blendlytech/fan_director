A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before your
close-up.

Something else on your mind?

Talk to us ↗
Is this for models or creators?+

Both. If fans ask you for personalized videos, greetings, styled scenes, or custom clips, Fan Director Studio gives those requests a clearer starting point. Bring your own audience and your own menu.

Do I need to change platforms?+

No. Share your boutique link wherever your fans already find you. Once you approve a request, payment and delivery stay on the platform you already use.

Do you take a cut of my custom videos?+

No. We don’t process fan payments or take a percentage of your customs. Your rates and your earnings stay yours.

Am I committing to every request?+

Never. A Scene Card is a request, not a confirmed order. You can approve that version, propose changes, ask a question, or decline. No price is final before your approval.

What can I put on my menu?+

At launch, menus support personal greetings, styled scenes, outfits, sets, and personal touches. Set your available options, prices, and boundaries during guided setup. Every request must follow our content rules and the rules of the platform you use.

What does the AI Director do?+

The AI Director is in final testing. It is designed to guide fans through your existing menu and prices. It does not set your rates, override your boundaries, accept a request, or replace your approval. Private shooting scripts are planned, not currently available.

What happens after the free Pro months?+

We’ll tell you the Pro price before your three free months end so you can decide. There is no card on file and no automatic charge. We ask founding creators for honest feedback; a testimonial is optional and only used with your permission.

How do I get started?+

Apply by email with the name you work under, where fans find you, and your usual custom-video rates. We’ll help you get your menu ready. Your fans can browse without creating an account.

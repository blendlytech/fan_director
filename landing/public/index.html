How it works

Your menu does the scoping. You make the call.

  1. We set you up

    Together we turn your rates into a menu: lengths, sets, outfits, add-ons and your limits.

  2. Share your link

    One boutique link for your bio, cam profile or pinned post. Fans browse without an account.

  3. Fans build a Scene Card

    They pick from your menu and watch the price add up, line by line. Anything off-menu reaches you unpriced.

  4. You decide

    Approve, propose changes, ask a question or decline. The fan pays you where you already get paid.

Always yours

You set every price and every limit.

Your prices, nobody else's

Every total is worked out from the prices you set. Nothing is invented, discounted or haggled.

Add-ons fans understand

Extra minutes, rush delivery, 4K and exclusive rights, each priced by you and explained on the card.

Limits up front

Mark things "ask me" or "hard no". Fans see them before they plan, so you stop saying no in DMs.

A floor no one can cross

Some requests are never allowed, whatever the menu says: anything involving minors, family roles or non-consent, among others.

Nothing happens without you

No request is accepted, and no price is final, until you approve that exact version.

We never touch your money

No cut of your customs. Fans pay you on OnlyFans, Fansly, ManyVids, in tokens, or however you take payment today.

Questions

Before you apply

Do you take a cut of my customs?

No. Fan Director Studio never handles commission payments. You agree the price on the Scene Card, the fan pays you on your own platform, and you mark it paid.

Do I have to leave my cam site or fan site?

No. It's a separate link that sits alongside them. You keep your audience, your payouts and your delivery exactly where they are.

What does the AI Director do, and what can't it do?

It helps a fan turn a vague idea into a Scene Card by suggesting options from your menu. It can't invent options, change your prices, get around your limits or approve anything. You approve every request yourself.

It's in final testing. Until it's switched on, fans build their card by hand from your menu.

Do fans need an account?

Not to browse your boutique. They sign in with their email to send you a request, so they can follow your answer.

What happens when my 3 free months end?

We'll tell you Pro's price before then. If you don't want it, you move to the Free plan. Nothing is charged automatically, because we never take your card.

How do I join?

Email us at info@studiolens.me with where you sell customs and roughly what you charge. We'll reply to set up a time to build your menu together.
